TASK 4: Mechanical Design Engineer

Objective:
Design the motorized mount and 3D-printed parts for the F30070M telescope.

Responsibilities:
- Design an Alt-Azimuth motorized mount.
- Develop the gear and motor coupling system.
- Create CAD models using Fusion 360.
- Evaluate stability, alignment, clearances, and mechanical loads.
- Prepare the assembly and 3D-printing instructions.

Deliverables:
- CAD source files.
- STL files for printing.
- Gear system design.
- Assembly guide.
- Mechanical stability assessment.
