SHARED RESOURCES

Purpose:
Store resources used by the whole F30070M smart telescope team.

Recommended practices:
- Meeting Notes: Record decisions, action items, owners, and deadlines.
- References: Store research links, datasheets, and technical documentation.
- Media: Store approved images, diagrams, and project photos.
- Final Presentation: Keep the approved presentation and related materials.

Each team member should place working files in their assigned folder and
share cross-team resources here when they are useful to everyone.
