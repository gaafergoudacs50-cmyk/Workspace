TASK 9: Power and Field Testing Engineer

Objective:
Plan the telescope power system and validate performance under field conditions.

Responsibilities:
- Evaluate battery and optional solar power solutions.
- Measure component power consumption.
- Prepare a power budget and runtime estimate.
- Plan field testing and calibration under different lighting conditions.
- Document environmental and operational limitations.

Deliverables:
- Power budget spreadsheet.
- Power consumption and runtime test results.
- Field test report with photos.
- Calibration documentation.
