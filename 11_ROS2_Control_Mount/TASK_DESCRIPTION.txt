TASK 12: ROS 2 Control Engineer

Objective:
Develop ROS-based control for the F30070M telescope mount.

Responsibilities:
- Create ROS 2 nodes for mount control and telemetry.
- Interface with motor drivers through an Arduino/ESP32 bridge as appropriate.
- Create a URDF model of the mount.
- Implement and validate relevant topics, including /joint_states and suitable motion commands.
- Develop an automatic tracking node and RViz visualization.

Suggested Stack:
- ROS 2 Humble, subject to operating-system compatibility.
- Arduino/ESP32 bridge.
- Compatible stepper motor drivers.
- URDF and RViz.

Deliverables:
- ROS 2 package.
- Launch files.
- URDF model.
- RViz demonstration video.
